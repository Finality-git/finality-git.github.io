Delete your Shapify account

Shapify by Finality Studios

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shapify and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Account.
  3. Under Cloud backup, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all backed-up data are erased immediately.

Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email finalityStudioltd@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Shapify account", sent from the email address you use to sign in to Shapify. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, hashed password and Google sign-in link.
  • All backed-up progress: profile details (age, gender, height, weight and goals), profile photo, steps, water, meals, workouts, weight history, XP, streaks, achievements and settings.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept on Shapify's servers after deletion.
  • Google Play keeps records of any purchases you made, as required for payments and tax. RevenueCat keeps purchase receipts linked to a random account ID (not your email) according to its privacy policy.
