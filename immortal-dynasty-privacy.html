Immortal Dynasty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Immortal Dynasty game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Immortal Dynasty is developed by Finality Studios. If you have questions, contact us at finalityStudioltd@gmail.com.

1. Playing without an account

You can play without an account. Your progress is then saved only on your phone, and we don't receive it.

2. Your Finality account

If you choose to bind your game to a Finality account, so that your progress is backed up and can be restored on another phone, we store:

  • your email address;
  • a securely hashed version of your password (we never store or see your actual password);
  • when the account was created and when you last logged in;
  • which account rewards you have claimed.

3. Your game save

With an account, your game save is backed up to our servers, along with a limited number of earlier copies so that progress can be recovered after a bad backup. A save holds your game progress, such as your character name and choices, realm, items and companions.

4. How we use this information

We use it only to run your account, back up and restore your progress, and keep the service working. We don't sell your information, and the game doesn't show ads.

5. Where it is stored

Account and save data is held on the hosting and database services we use to run the game's servers, which may be outside your country. Data is sent to our servers over an encrypted connection.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all of its cloud saves in the game at any time, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account doesn't remove the progress saved on your phone; uninstalling the game removes that.

7. Children

Immortal Dynasty isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
