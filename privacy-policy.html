Shapify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Shapify app ("Shapify", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Shapify is developed by Finality Studios. If you have questions, contact us at finalityStudioltd@gmail.com.

1. Your Shapify account

To use Shapify you create an account with your email address and a password, or with Google sign-in. Your account lets us back up your progress and restore it when you log in on another phone.

  • Email and password: we store your email and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store or see your actual password.
  • Google sign-in: we receive your Google account ID, email address and name from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

2. Information we store

The information you enter in Shapify, and the progress the app records, is kept on your phone and backed up to your account on our servers:

  • Profile details: name, email address, age, gender, height, current and target weight, goals, and lifestyle answers from sign-up (such as activity level, budget range, wake and sleep times).
  • Optional profile photo you choose from your device.
  • Activity and health-related records: step counts, water intake, meals logged, workouts, weight history, XP, streaks and achievements.
  • App settings such as theme and reminder preferences, and which supporter tier you own.

We use this information only to run Shapify for you: to personalise your plans, show your progress, and keep your progress safe across phones.

3. Where your data is kept and how it is protected

  • Your account and backed-up progress are stored in a MongoDB Atlas cloud database, and our app server is hosted by Render. These providers may store data on servers outside Nigeria.
  • Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Only Finality Studios can access our servers, and only to operate and support Shapify.
  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and backed-up progress are permanently erased from our database.

4. Device permissions

  • Physical activity — to count your steps using your phone's built-in step sensor, including in the background when automatic tracking is on.
  • Notifications, alarms and Do Not Disturb — to send water and meal reminders, wake-up alarms, and to optionally turn on Do Not Disturb during your sleep time.
  • Run at start-up — to restore your reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Internet — to back up your progress, sign you in, show ads and process purchases.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users may choose to watch rewarded video ads to unlock features. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), Shapify will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

6. In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Supporter tiers are one-time purchases processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To verify purchases and restore them on a new phone, we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipts and your Shapify account ID (a random identifier, not your email). See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your profile, weight or health records with advertisers.
  • We do not share your data with third parties other than the service providers named in this policy (MongoDB Atlas, Render, Google, and RevenueCat), and only so they can provide their service to us.

8. Health information disclaimer

Shapify provides general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional before starting a new diet or exercise programme.

9. Children

Shapify is not intended for children under 13, and the app does not allow sign-up below that age.

10. Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can edit your profile at any time in the app's Settings.
  • You can sign out in Settings → Account. Your progress stays saved in your account.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all backed-up data in Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • If you no longer have the app, you can request account deletion here.
  • You can turn off notifications, alarms and automatic step tracking in the app or in Android settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
