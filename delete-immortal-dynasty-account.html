Delete Your Immortal Dynasty Account

Immortal Dynasty is developed by Finality Studios. You can delete your account in the game, or ask us to do it.

In the game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Find the Bind Account section and log in if you need to.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email finalityStudioltd@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Immortal Dynasty account". We'll delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account (email address and hashed password), your rewards record, and all of your cloud saves, including earlier copies. Nothing from the account is kept after deletion.

Progress saved on your phone isn't touched by deleting your account. Uninstalling the game removes it.
