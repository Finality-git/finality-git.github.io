The studio

Made in Nigeria.
Made to be played.

Finality Studios is an independent studio building games and apps from Nigeria for players everywhere. We design, build and publish everything ourselves, from the first sketch to the store listing.

We started with two very different products: an epic cultivation RPG and a friendly fitness companion. What they share is care: worlds and tools that feel good to come back to.

01Worlds first

Every product starts with a feeling we want to give people, then everything we build serves it.

02Built for real phones

Fast and smooth on the devices and networks people actually use, not only the newest flagship.

03Respect for players

Clear privacy, fair design and no dark patterns. Your data and your time matter.
