Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Finality Studios LTD (RC 9593980), a company incorporated in Nigeria ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects personal data when you use our website, mobile applications and games (together, the "Services"). We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws.

Each of our apps also has its own privacy policy, which takes priority for that app: Shapify Privacy Policy · Immortal Dynasty Privacy Policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: such as your name, email address and login details, if you create an account.
  • Fitness data (fitness app): workout logs, goals, progress and, if you choose to provide them, body measurements such as height and weight.
  • Gameplay data (games): progress, scores, achievements and in-game purchase records.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, crash reports and how you interact with the Services.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team.
  • Beta accounts: your name, email address, a password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form), Discord username (if you give it) and which products you want to test, when you create a beta account on our beta page. If you are approved, we also store the projects you join and the reviews, bug reports (including any device details you add) and suggestions you send us.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To save your progress and sync it across devices.
  • To respond to support requests and send important service notices.
  • To detect, prevent and fix fraud, abuse, bugs and security issues.
  • To review beta requests, email you about the result, give approved testers access to test builds, and use your feedback to fix and improve our products. We do not add beta testers to marketing lists.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and improving the Services, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Services (for example, cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, app store payment processing and the database host that stores beta accounts), under agreements that protect your data, or where required by law.

5. Health and fitness data

Fitness data is used only to deliver features of the fitness app. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties for their marketing. If the app connects to Apple Health or Google Health Connect, it does so only with your permission, and you can revoke that permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Beta accounts and the feedback sent with them are kept while the beta program runs; you can delete your beta account and everything sent from it at any time from your beta dashboard, or by emailing us from your beta email address. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email finalitystudioltd@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

Our Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we take steps to make sure your data stays protected as required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above.
